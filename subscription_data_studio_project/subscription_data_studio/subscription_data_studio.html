Billing rules applied

Trial and renewal7-day trial; first charge at trial end. Renew every 30, 90, or 365 days from first successful payment.
Price and discount$24.99 / $69.99 / $274.99. `15OFF` can reduce only the first paid period.
Failure handlingFreeze on decline; retry on days 1, 3, and 7. Successful recovery preserves the renewal anchor.
Zipcode taxIllustrative tax is calculated on gross after discount, recorded separately from net, and reversed with refunds.
Multiple subscriptionsA user can reactivate after cancellation; the prior subscription is cancelled before the new lifecycle. No user has more than one active subscription.
